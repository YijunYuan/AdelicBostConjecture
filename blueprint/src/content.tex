\chapter*{Tensor semistability over number fields}
The objective is the maximal-slope identity for Hermitian bundles over the
ring of integers of an arbitrary number field. The route follows
\cite{bost-specht-manuscript}: a sublinear covolume estimate for rectangular
Specht lattices gives a geometric slope inequality; an extremal metric
argument then removes geometric semistability. The final passage to
arbitrary factors uses Harder--Narasimhan filtrations.

Throughout, $K$ is a number field, $\Ocal=\Ocal_K$, $D=[K:\mathbb Q]$,
and $\Delta_K$ is the absolute discriminant. The set of archimedean places
contains one representative of each complex conjugate pair. Its weights are
$w_v=1$ at real places and $w_v=2$ at complex places, so $\sum_v w_v=D$.
All tensor products of Hermitian spaces carry the Hilbert tensor metric;
subspaces carry induced metrics and quotients carry quotient metrics.
All logarithms are natural.
