This document concerns proper adelic curves over countable fields of
characteristic zero. At an Archimedean place the absolute value is the usual
real or complex absolute value. Tensor products carry the Hilbert tensor norm
at Archimedean places and the injective tensor norm at the other places.
The central assertion is additivity of maximal slopes for positive-rank
Hermitian adelic vector bundles, as in \cite{bost2026}.
